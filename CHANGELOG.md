# Changelog

All notable changes to the Tatanka Trading free indicators. Versions match the Toolkit's dashboard header and the version line at the top of each `.cs` file.

## Updates to release v1.0.0

- **November 8, 2026: AMT Toolkit v1.2.2** (TradingView). The same fixes as the NinjaTrader v1.2.2, where TradingView allows them: every pd line (pdHigh, pdLow, pdOpen, pdPOC, pdVAH, pdVAL) switches to the session just closed at the 3 PM CT close, together, drawn from that bar (the pdHigh and pdLow lines kept yesterday's price until 5 PM while their labels and alerts had switched); OVN INVENTORY is the count at the last overnight bar; the prior month's value comes from the month's 1-minute bars, so it matches the Monthly profile and nPOC; alerts only for lines that are drawn; Poor High/Low at the session's high and low, and single prints filled from below; Enable IB or OVN off leave the DAY TYPE; GC and CL rows match the profile; level zones use the Daily ATR Period; a later RTH Session for VA decides OPEN, the gap test and the 80% Rule together; RTH-only charts roll the prior day's high, low and open. Profiles built from every trade stay NinjaTrader-only (Pine reads 1-minute bars). Alerts created before the update keep running the old version: delete and re-create them.
- **November 8, 2026: AMT Toolkit v1.2.2** (NinjaTrader 8). The current profile is now built from every trade when **11. Volume Profile → Profile Period** is Session or Weekly (the default): its ticks are downloaded from the start of the session or week as the chart loads, then taken live, so the profile, its POC and value area and the Developing POC, VAH and VAL lines are a tick volume profile's instead of 1-minute bars spread over their range. **11. Volume Profile → Profile From Ticks** turns it off. Monthly and Yearly profiles, older weeks and any minute whose ticks are missing still use 1-minute bars. Also, every pd line now switches to the session that just closed at its close (15:00 CT by default), together: pdHigh, pdLow, pdOpen, pdPOC, pdVAH and pdVAL, and that session's profile is redrawn from its ticks right then. Before, the profile and the value lines kept their old values until the 17:00 open, all weekend after a Friday, and pdHigh and pdLow had switched two hours before the value (pdPOC could sit below "pdLow"). From the close to the 17:00 open the new lines start at the close and the chart keeps the old ones over the day before it; today's 80% Rule, the OPEN row and the day type stay measured against the value in force at today's open. Also fixed:
  1. **OVN INVENTORY** shows the count at the last overnight bar (08:29), not the 08:15 reading, so it no longer says TRAP after the trap resolved.
  2. **Prior month's value** (pmPOC, pmVAH, pmVAL) comes from the month's 1-minute bars when the chart holds the whole month, at the profile's rows, so it matches the Monthly profile and the Monthly nPOC (on NQ they were 93 points apart). Shorter charts use the 30-minute bars as before.
  3. **Alerts** only watch lines that are drawn: OVN High/Low stop at **Delete OVN Lines At** (15:00), and a section switched off (Previous Day, Previous Week, Previous Month, Volume Profile, a single line) no longer alerts.
  4. **Poor High/Low** sit at the session's high and low (the Poor High was one tick above it) and are repaired by a trade one tick beyond. A single print fills when its price trades from below too.
  5. **Enable IB** and **Enable OVN High/Low** off no longer change the DAY TYPE.
  6. **GC:** the prior day's and month's value use the profile's 0.5 rows, so labels match the lines (3414.25 was labelled 3414.3).
  7. **Level zones** in ATR % mode use the **Daily ATR Period**, as the dashboard does (always 14 before), and the Zone Entry alert shows the zone's width unrounded (±1.12, not ±1.00).
  8. **RTH Session for VA** starting after 08:30: OPEN, the gap test and the 80% Rule all use its first bar's open (the 80% RULE row stayed "---" while the fill was drawn).
  9. **CL:** the weekly and monthly nPOC use the profile's rows, so they sit on the drawn profile's POC.

  Download `TatankaAmtToolkit_NT8.zip` (or `TatankaAll_NT8.zip`).
- **November 8, 2026: AMT Toolkit v1.2.1** (NinjaTrader 8). The prior day's profile now matches its pdPOC, pdVAH and pdVAL lines. ## v1.0.0 (October 2026)

First public release, free and open source under MPL 2.0, on NinjaTrader 8 and TradingView: Tatanka AMT Toolkit, TPO Profile, If/Then Bias Table and HTF EMA (1hr 21 EMA).

### Tatanka AMT Toolkit

- One name on every platform: **Tatanka AMT Toolkit**, dashboard header "Tatanka AMT v1.0.0".
- Organized around the auction questions: Where is value? Balanced or trending? Who's trapped? What's unfinished? Which reference prices matter? Where are my levels?
- Volume profile for the session, week (default), month or year; prior-day and prior-month value; RTH VWAP.
- Initial balance, Dalton day-type classification, open location, 80% Rule.
- Overnight inventory versus settlement with trap detection; late-day spike base.
- Naked POCs (daily, weekly, monthly), single prints, poor highs and lows.
- Reference prices: overnight, prior day, week and month highs and lows; weekly and RTH open.
- Paste-your-own levels box that reads the one-line format `YYYY-MM-DD, pivot, prices…` (also the labeled Pivot / Resistance / Support block), with zones, flip coloring and alerts.
- Profile Row Scale (Auto) sizes profile rows to the symbol, replacing the old High Volatility Mode.
- NinjaTrader: levels file with `{root}` token, STALE and wrong-symbol checks, zone-entry alert mode, optional Discord webhook for phone alerts.
- Late spike base reads the same 1-minute close on history and live (TradingView).
- Removed: the buy/sell signal engine, scoring and playbook signals from earlier private versions.

### TPO Profile, If/Then Bias Table, HTF EMA

- NinjaTrader 8 ports of the TradingView scripts, with MPL 2.0 headers.
- NinjaTrader class names are Tatanka-prefixed (`TatankaTpoProfile`, `TatankaHtfEma`, `TatankaIfThenBiasTable`) so they can't clash with other scripts on your PC.
